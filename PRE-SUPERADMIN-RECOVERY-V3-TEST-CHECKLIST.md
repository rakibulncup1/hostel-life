# V3 Functional Checklist

1. Manager request inbox: submit a normal request from a member; confirm it appears without manual page refresh.
2. Correction after cutoff: edit a past target date and confirm the correction request is accepted and visible to manager.
3. Manager correction approval before 9 PM: approve and confirm late card appears for everyone.
4. After 9 PM: confirm late card disappears while history remains.
5. Manager edit of an approved meal request: before 9 PM it remains planned/pending; after 9 PM it is final immediately.
6. Month close: existing historical requests do not block; only future requests beyond selected close date trigger warning.
7. Market entry: select active member, add items, save, then verify history/detail.
8. Reports: normal authenticated member can load/download report without manager-only error.
9. Khala: manager adds an entry and all members see it after online refresh.
10. Dashboard: late card appears directly under main meal card only when active visible late events exist.
11. Offline: visit online first, then disable network; cached dashboard/meal/late data remains visible.
12. SuperAdmin is intentionally not part of this phase.
